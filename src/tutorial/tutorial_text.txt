Welcome to the Tutorial!
Use WASD to move and space
to jump.

If at any point you become
stuck, press O to restart.
.
The grey tile on the floor
ahead is a freeze tile. Upon
touching it you will be unable
to move for 1 second.

Avoid becoming fully trapped in
it.
.
The darker grey tile ahead is
a teleporter (from) tile. It will 
teleport you to a nearby
teleporter (to) tile.

Use a double jump to help cross
the teleporters.
.
Use right click to use your
grappling hook. The grappling
hook only works on the wooden
hookable tiles, and other players.

Try it out here!
.
The hook-through tiles, as
suggested by the name, are able
to be hooked through.

Use it to cross the gap.
.
The red tiles will respawn
you at the beginning of the
level if you touch them.

Do not touch them at any cost.
.
You can use the grenade launcher
to give yourself or others a boost.
You can also cause it to explode
by hitting it with the bat.

Use the mouse to aim and left
click to shoot. Use 1 and 2 to
switch between weapons.
.
Press jump right before shooting
the ground to give you a burst
of upwards speed!

Use this to climb the wall.
.
The tiles shown in the box are
the start and finish line tiles.

The green one starts the timer 
and the red one ends it. Try to
reach the finish line as quick
as you can.
.
Now test what you've learnt and
complete the level as quickly
as you can.

Good luck.
.
